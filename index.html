Our Story

Crafted with care,
served with soul

Born from a simple belief — that exceptional food doesn't need to be complicated. Tucked in the heart of Mokopane, we've been turning fresh, locally-sourced ingredients into unforgettable plates since 2012.

Our chefs treat every service like a performance — seasonal menus, hand-stretched dough, slow-braised cuts, and desserts that linger long after the last bite.

Farm Fresh
Ingredients sourced from local Limpopo farmers weekly.
Wood Fired
Authentic wood-fired ovens for real depth of flavour.
Curated Wines
A rotating list of SA boutique estate wines.
Chef-Led
Every dish signed off by our executive kitchen team.
